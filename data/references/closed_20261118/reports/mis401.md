## 3. The exact MIS model

### 3.1 Candidates

For a target side L:

1. **Axis lattice A** — all unit squares with lower-left corner at (i, j),
   i,j ∈ ℤ, fully inside [0,L]². At L ∈ (20.5, 20.533] this is 20×20 = 400.
2. **Axis lattice B** — same with lower-left at (i+Δ, j+Δ), Δ = L−20
   (the bank's second phase; 400 cells). Other Δ values (0.25, 0.5, 0.75,
   single-axis offsets) are swept as variants.
3. **Rotated patch** — centres on the unit tiling lattice
   `c = (ox,oy) + i·u(θ) + j·v(θ)`, `u=(cosθ,sinθ)`, `v=(−sinθ,cosθ)`, fully
   inside the box; θ swept around 69.32° ± 1.5° in 0.1° steps and over the whole
   [55°, 90°] range at 0.25°, offsets (ox,oy) on a 0.125 grid (0.05 in the fine
   pass). At θ = 69.32° the full box lattice has 371 cells; the 18×13 band
   restriction has at most 234.
4. **Third phase** — (a) a second axis phase as in 2 with a distinct offset;
   (b) a second rotated lattice at a distinct angle (θ ± 0.3°, ± 0.6°, and the
   bank's eight measured angles); (c) multi-segment bands: v-quartile bands at
   independently chosen angles from the bank's set; (d) the bank's own 197 patch
   squares as fixed candidates.

### 3.2 Conflict graph

Vertices are candidates. An edge joins two candidates whose **interiors**
overlap. The test is exact SAT: for squares at angles t₁,t₂ the four separating
axes are u(t₁), v(t₁), u(t₂), v(t₂); projections are intervals of half-width
`0.5(|cos(t−φ)|+|sin(t−φ)|)`; a pair is separated if any axis has
`|d·n| ≥ r₁+r₂`. **Touching is allowed** (gap 0 is not an edge). The matrix is
built vectorised over the bbox-prefiltered pair list (1.5–2.3k vertices,
5k–23k edges) and every candidate is also checked for containment.

### 3.3 Solvers

* **Two phases (A vs B)** — bipartite, so the exact maximum independent set is
  `|A| + |B| − maximum_matching` (König). Use
  `scipy.sparse.csgraph.maximum_bipartite_matching`. This **proves** the 400
  values in §4.1.
* **Three/four phases** — set packing MILP
  `max Σxᵢ s.t. xᵢ+xⱼ ≤ 1 ∀ edges, x ∈ {0,1}`, solved with HiGHS via
  `scipy.optimize.milp` (sparse, presolve on). Optimality status is recorded.
* **Bank patch fixed** — the remaining axis sub-problem is again bipartite
  (A vs B after deleting cells that hit the fixed patch), so König is exact.
* **Heuristic lower bounds** — seeded greedy + 1-for-2 local search, 20–40
  restarts, to warm-start the MILP and to screen parameter grids.

Every returned set is re-checked with explicit pairwise SAT at tolerance 0
(penetration must be ≤ 0), plus containment, plus `n == len(emitted)` and
`n ≤ L²`.

## 5. Why the bank's 401 is not reachable by the lattice families

1. **Area/axis bound.** Any axis-only packing at L < 21 has at most
   ⌊L⌋² = 400 squares. So a 401 needs rotated squares.
2. **Single rotated lattice.** A full rotated lattice has ≤ 400 cells (max at
   θ=0); combined with the full axis lattice, every rotated cell conflicts with
   an axis cell, so König gives 400.
3. **Two rotated angles / three phases.** The MILP optimum is still 400 for all
   tested parameter grids. The extra phase adds cells but they are inside the
   region already covered by the axis union.
4. **The bank's 401 is a relaxed layout.** Its band is not a rigid lattice: 26
   near-lattice rows of individually angled squares (angles spread over 3.3°),
   plus **24 individual slivers** at 0.3–0.7°. Keeping the band and
   re-optimising the axis part exactly gives 385; the slivers supply the
   remaining 16. So the third phase that matters is a *continuum of hand
   placements in the 0.5-wide interface*, not another lattice.
5. **The 400-square layout at the record's side is saturated.** A continuous
   void scan (0.02 grid, all orientations) finds no unit square that can be
   added; its largest empty disk has radius 0.560. Adding the 401st requires a
   rearrangement, not a refill.
6. **The bank is first-order jammed.** A global translation-only contact LP over
   all 401 squares (1203 free variables, 801 contacts, box side variable) is
   infeasible for any shrink ≥ 2×10⁻⁷. Wall slack is ≤ 7.3×10⁻⁴ on 3 of 66
   wall squares and zero on the other 63. Removing any one square (tested on 19
   representatives including all slivers and 6 band squares) allows a shrink of
   only 6.1×10⁻⁸. Sliding any single band row by up to ±0.5 allows 0. So no
   local deformation, discrete slide, or one-square removal unlocks the
   0.006876 needed for the record.

The record's 20.526135333 therefore requires a **different relaxed layout**,
i.e. a bespoke global construction/search over individual square placements
(what the record team's "many slightly different angles" prose describes), not
a maximum independent set over lattice phases. That is a qualitatively larger
search than the one this brief proposed, and it is the honest reason this track
has no certified candidate.
